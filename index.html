Local-first IRS & attorney case management

Your entire IRS audit, in one managed case — on your own machine.

Aldyn reads your Gmail over IMAP and turns every inbound attorney and IRS message — body, subject and attachments — into an organized case: filed to the right business and attorney, with each ask tracked as a deadline and a reply already drafted. Postgres and Redis run in Docker on your Mac. The AI brain is Claude Desktop, on your own subscription. No hosted database, no per-token bill.
