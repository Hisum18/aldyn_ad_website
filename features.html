Everything on one machine

The audit stops living in your inbox.

A notice arrives, an attorney replies, an IDR lands with four separate asks buried in a paragraph. Aldyn pulls that apart the moment it arrives and puts every piece where it belongs — so the case file assembles itself while you work.

Every inbox, one thread

Connect any number of Gmail accounts. All of them flow into one threaded Mail view you can filter by account, attorney, business, tag, direction or sender. Reply, Reply All, Forward and Compose all send over real SMTP — on your click.

Cases that assemble themselves

Each notice becomes a case — CP504, CP2000, LT11, a Form 4564 IDR — carrying its own action items, full email timeline, extracted documents and pending drafts. Nothing is filed in two places.

Deadlines pulled out of the mail

Every ask in an attorney email becomes one tracked item — document request, question, action, or FYI. Overdue and due-this-week surface on Today, with urgency colours and an internal date ahead of the real one.

Defense check

An independent second read of the case file: scope plus tax-law analysis, run against your engagement letter, flagging what a request reaches beyond. It is a second opinion to raise with your representation — not a legal opinion.

Documents you can actually find

Every IRS notice and attachment — PDF, DOCX, image, ZIP — is extracted on arrival and indexed with Postgres full-text search. Ask for the 2023 bank statements and get the file, not a folder.

Drafted, never sent

The AI files mail, extracts deadlines, summarizes threads and writes the reply. It cannot send. Every outbound message waits in Aldyn's composer until you read it and hit send yourself.
